Controlled vocabulary for kinds of syncretic theological relationships between deities.

Concept scheme: https://cair-nepal.org/heritagegraph/scheme/SyncreticType

ValueIRIDefinition
Equivalencehttps://cair-nepal.org/heritagegraph/SyncreticType#EquivalenceClaimed identity: the two are held to be one deity in different traditions (e.g. Avalokiteshvara and Matsyendranath for Newar Buddhists).
Appropriationhttps://cair-nepal.org/heritagegraph/SyncreticType#AppropriationClaimed adoption: one tradition is held to have taken the deity over from the other.
Fusionhttps://cair-nepal.org/heritagegraph/SyncreticType#FusionClaimed combination: the deity is held to combine features of deities of several traditions.
Historicalhttps://cair-nepal.org/heritagegraph/SyncreticType#HistoricalClaimed historical derivation: the deity is held to have developed from the other over time.
