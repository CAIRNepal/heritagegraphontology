# Concepts

### Abhisheka

**IRI:** `https://cair-nepal.org/heritagegraph/RitualType#Abhisheka`

Ritual bathing/anointing of deity.

### Alienation of an endowment

**IRI:** `https://cair-nepal.org/heritagegraph/EndowmentEventType#Alienation`

Loss of endowed property to private or other ownership (sale, encroachment, registration in a private name).

### Appropriation

**IRI:** `https://cair-nepal.org/heritagegraph/SyncreticType#Appropriation`

Claimed adoption: one tradition is held to have taken the deity over from the other.

### Augmentation of an endowment

**IRI:** `https://cair-nepal.org/heritagegraph/EndowmentEventType#Augmentation`

Addition of land or other property to an existing endowment.

### Bhajan

**IRI:** `https://cair-nepal.org/heritagegraph/RitualType#Bhajan`

Devotional singing ritual.

### Century

**IRI:** `https://cair-nepal.org/heritagegraph/DatePrecision#Century`

Within century range.

### Chaitya style ~~deprecated~~

**IRI:** `https://cair-nepal.org/heritagegraph/ArchitecturalStyle#Chaitya`

Buddhist votive shrine style.

### Chariot Procession

**IRI:** `https://cair-nepal.org/heritagegraph/RitualType#ChariotProcession`

Ritual chariot pulling.

### Circa

**IRI:** `https://cair-nepal.org/heritagegraph/DatePrecision#Circa`

Approximate date.

### Circumambulation

**IRI:** `https://cair-nepal.org/heritagegraph/RitualType#Circumambulation`

Ritual circular movement around sacred site.

### Community Use Only

**IRI:** `https://cair-nepal.org/heritagegraph/AccessProtocol#CommunityUseOnly`

For use within the community concerned; not for general publication.

### Damaged

**IRI:** `https://cair-nepal.org/heritagegraph/ConditionType#Damaged`

Partially damaged.

### Decade

**IRI:** `https://cair-nepal.org/heritagegraph/DatePrecision#Decade`

Within 10-year range.

### Deinstallation Ritual

**IRI:** `https://cair-nepal.org/heritagegraph/RitualType#DeinstallationRitual`

De-installation/conclusion ritual.

### Deity guthi

**IRI:** `https://cair-nepal.org/heritagegraph/GuthiType#TempleGuthi`

Deity guthi (dyah guthi): an association, usually endowed with land, that keeps up the worship and the festival of a deity at its temple or shrine.

### Destroyed

**IRI:** `https://cair-nepal.org/heritagegraph/ExistenceStatus#Destroyed`

Known to have been destroyed; no physical remains.

### Dome style

**IRI:** `https://cair-nepal.org/heritagegraph/ArchitecturalStyle#Dome`

Dome-based style (Mughal/Neo-classical influence).

### Equivalence

**IRI:** `https://cair-nepal.org/heritagegraph/SyncreticType#Equivalence`

Claimed identity: the two are held to be one deity in different traditions (e.g. Avalokiteshvara and Matsyendranath for Newar Buddhists).

### Exact

**IRI:** `https://cair-nepal.org/heritagegraph/DatePrecision#Exact`

Precise date known.

### Extant

**IRI:** `https://cair-nepal.org/heritagegraph/ExistenceStatus#Extant`

Currently exists in physical form.

### Founding of an endowment

**IRI:** `https://cair-nepal.org/heritagegraph/EndowmentEventType#Founding`

Dedication of land or other property that creates an endowment (and usually its Guthi).

### Fusion

**IRI:** `https://cair-nepal.org/heritagegraph/SyncreticType#Fusion`

Claimed combination: the deity is held to combine features of deities of several traditions.

### Good

**IRI:** `https://cair-nepal.org/heritagegraph/ConditionType#Good`

No significant damage.

### Historical

**IRI:** `https://cair-nepal.org/heritagegraph/SyncreticType#Historical`

Claimed historical derivation: the deity is held to have developed from the other over time.

### Homa

**IRI:** `https://cair-nepal.org/heritagegraph/RitualType#Homa`

Fire offering ritual.

### Hypothetical

**IRI:** `https://cair-nepal.org/heritagegraph/ExistenceStatus#Hypothetical`

Theorized (e.g. a reconstruction on paper only); never physically realized.

### Initiates Only

**IRI:** `https://cair-nepal.org/heritagegraph/AccessProtocol#InitiatesOnly`

Restricted to initiates (e.g. the agam deity and rites of a lineage or monastery).

### Installation Ritual

**IRI:** `https://cair-nepal.org/heritagegraph/RitualType#InstallationRitual`

Installation/enshrinement ritual.

### Jatra

**IRI:** `https://cair-nepal.org/heritagegraph/RitualType#Jatra`

Festival procession ritual.

### Jatra Guthi

**IRI:** `https://cair-nepal.org/heritagegraph/GuthiType#JatraGuthi`

Festival organization trust.

### Kamya Puja

**IRI:** `https://cair-nepal.org/heritagegraph/RitualType#KamyaPuja`

Desire-based optional worship.

### Lost

**IRI:** `https://cair-nepal.org/heritagegraph/ExistenceStatus#Lost`

Existence documented but location/remains unknown.

### Masked Performance

**IRI:** `https://cair-nepal.org/heritagegraph/RitualType#MaskedPerformance`

Ritual masked dance.

### Month

**IRI:** `https://cair-nepal.org/heritagegraph/DatePrecision#Month`

Month-level precision (e.g. a lunar month of a given year).

### Naimittika Puja

**IRI:** `https://cair-nepal.org/heritagegraph/RitualType#NaimittikaPuja`

Occasional/Festival worship.

### Nasha Guthi

**IRI:** `https://cair-nepal.org/heritagegraph/GuthiType#NashaGuthi`

Music and dance trust.

### Nationalisation of an endowment

**IRI:** `https://cair-nepal.org/heritagegraph/EndowmentEventType#Nationalisation`

Transfer of an endowment to state administration (e.g. the Guthi Sansthan, established 1964 CE / VS 2021).

### Nitya Puja

**IRI:** `https://cair-nepal.org/heritagegraph/RitualType#NityaPuja`

Daily mandatory worship.

### Open

**IRI:** `https://cair-nepal.org/heritagegraph/AccessProtocol#Open`

No community restriction on access or publication.

### Pagoda style

**IRI:** `https://cair-nepal.org/heritagegraph/ArchitecturalStyle#Pagoda`

Multi-tiered roof form of the Kathmandu Valley, as in the Newar tiered temple: roofs that diminish upward and rest on carved struts (also used on other building types).

### Partially Extant

**IRI:** `https://cair-nepal.org/heritagegraph/ExistenceStatus#PartiallyExtant`

Fragments or ruins remain.

### Procession Ritual

**IRI:** `https://cair-nepal.org/heritagegraph/RitualType#ProcessionRitual`

Ritual procession/movement.

### Processional Movement

**IRI:** `https://cair-nepal.org/heritagegraph/RitualType#ProcessionalMovement`

General ritual movement between locations.

### Puja Guthi

**IRI:** `https://cair-nepal.org/heritagegraph/GuthiType#PujaGuthi`

Daily worship trust.

### Raj Guthi

**IRI:** `https://cair-nepal.org/heritagegraph/GuthiType#RajGuthi`

Severely damaged or collapsed.

### San Guthi ~~deprecated~~

**IRI:** `https://cair-nepal.org/heritagegraph/GuthiType#SanGuthi`

### Sana Guthi

**IRI:** `https://cair-nepal.org/heritagegraph/GuthiType#SanaGuthi`

Funeral association (sanah guthi): the association that organises the funeral procession and cremation of member households; in some localities it and the si guthi are one body.

### Seasonal

**IRI:** `https://cair-nepal.org/heritagegraph/AccessProtocol#Seasonal`

Access or use only at certain times (e.g. during a festival).

### Secret Sacred

**IRI:** `https://cair-nepal.org/heritagegraph/AccessProtocol#SecretSacred`

Secret or sacred knowledge with specific conditions of access.

### Shikhara style

### Si Guthi

**IRI:** `https://cair-nepal.org/heritagegraph/GuthiType#SiGuthi`

Death guthi (si guthi): the association whose members carry out the cremation and death rites of member households.
