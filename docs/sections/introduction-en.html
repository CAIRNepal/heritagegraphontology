Introduction back to ToC

HeritageGraph (https://w3id.org/heritagegraph/ontology) is an event-centric ontology for Nepalese living heritage, aligned with CIDOC-CRM, CRMinf, PROV-O, GeoSPARQL, OWL-Time, and Europeana EDM. It models temples, rituals, Guthi institutions, syncretic deity relations, and assertion-level provenance for multi-vocal heritage knowledge graphs. Release version 1.0.0. Source and evaluation artefacts: https://github.com/CAIRNepal/heritagegraphontology.
