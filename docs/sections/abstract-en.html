Abstract

HeritageGraph is an event-centric OWL ontology for describing Nepalese living heritage — its tangible objects, sacred places, deities, rituals, festivals, and the communities and institutions (notably the *Guthi*) that sustain them. Rather than attaching facts directly to objects, the model represents history as a web of events (production, ritual, custody transfer, documentation) so that spatial, temporal, and participatory relationships can be queried and reasoned over. Every assertion can carry its provenance and epistemic standing, allowing competing or multi-vocal sources to coexist. The ontology is generated from a LinkML schema, is logically consistent (DL expressivity *ALCIQ(D)*), and is aligned with CIDOC‑CRM 7.2.1, CRMinf, PROV‑O, and the Europeana Data Model (EDM) to maximise interoperability with the wider cultural-heritage Linked Data cloud.
