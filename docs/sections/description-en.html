HeritageGraph: Description back to ToC

HeritageGraph follows an **event-centric** modelling pattern inherited from CIDOC-CRM: instead of linking attributes directly to a heritage object, the ontology connects objects, places, agents, and time-spans *through events*. This makes provenance, sequence, and participation explicit and queryable. The schema defines **66 classes** and **134 properties** (**102 object**, **32 datatype**), publishes **7 enumerations** as SKOS concept schemes (**51** controlled-vocabulary concepts), and reuses a further **18 classes** from CIDOC-CRM, CRMinf, PROV-O, and SKOS with local axioms. It is generated from a LinkML source of truth (`HeritageGraph.yaml`). ### Core building blocks - **Physical heritage things** — `PhysicalHeritageThing` (a union of `ArchitecturalStructure`, `IconographicObject`, and `ArchitecturalElement`) covers temples (`Temple`, `Stupa`, `Chaitya`, `ReligiousStructure`), *murti*/*paubha* icons (`Murti`, `Paubha`), water structures (`WaterStructure`, `DhungeDhara`, `Pokhari`), and rest houses (`RestHouse`, `Pati`, `Sattal`, `Dharmashala`). - **Events** — heritage change is represented through typed events: production (via CIDOC-CRM `crm:E12_Production`), `Consecration`, `Enshrinement`, custody transfer, `RitualEvent` (e.g. `ChariotFestival`, `MaskedDance`) grouped by `Festival`, `HistoricalEvent`, and the living-goddess lifecycle (`KumariSelectionEvent`, `KumariEnthronementEvent`, `KumariTenure`, `KumariRetirementEvent`, generalised by `KumariLifecycleEvent`). - **Actors & institutions** — actors and persons (via CIDOC-CRM `crm:E39_Actor` / `crm:E21_Person`), `CasteGroup`, `DataCustodian`, and the institution central to Nepalese heritage, `Guthi` (typed by the `GuthiType` enumeration). - **Places & time** — places and time-spans (via `crm:E53_Place` / `crm:E52_Time-Span`) and `CalendarSystem` support multi-calendar dating (e.g. Bikram Sambat, Nepal Sambat, Gregorian) with explicit `DatePrecision`. - **Knowledge & provenance** — assertions and beliefs (aligned with CRMinf `I2_Belief` / `Proposition` and PROV-O), `DataSource` (`ArchivalRecord`, `OralHistoryRecording`, `FieldSurveyDataset`), `DocumentationActivity`, `FieldSurveyActivity`, and `Verification` capture *who asserted what, from which source, and with what confidence*, so competing sources can coexist. - **Syncretism** — `SyncreticRelationship` links `Deity` instances that bridge Hindu and Buddhist traditions (`ReligiousTradition`), typed by the `SyncreticType` enumeration — as a provenance-qualified interpretive assertion rather than semantic identity (`owl:sameAs`). ### How the pieces relate A typical pattern looks like this: an `ArchitecturalStructure` was produced by a production event (`crm:E12_Production`) that took place at a place (`crm:E53_Place`), occurred during a time-span (`crm:E52_Time-Span`), and had a `Person` or `Guthi` as participant. A `RitualEvent` activates the same structure during a `Festival`, following a canonical sequence. Each fact can be recorded as a source-attributed belief (CRMinf `I2_Belief`) that was derived from a `DataSource` and checked by a `Verification`, so contradictory claims (e.g. two construction dates) coexist with their evidence and epistemic standing. ### Interoperability modules The core vocabulary carries its external alignments **inline** (SKOS `exactMatch` / `closeMatch` / `broadMatch`, `rdfs:subClassOf`, and `rdfs:subPropertyOf`) to CIDOC-CRM, CRMinf, PROV-O, Getty AAT, schema.org, Wikidata, and a Europeana EDM projection. Companion artefacts (in `ontology/`): - `HeritageGraph.shacl.ttl` — SHACL validation shapes for the core vocabulary (75 node shapes). - `lux/heritagegraph-lux-alignment.ttl` — a bridge to Yale's LUX / Linked Art, with its shapes in `lux/HeritageGraph-lux.shacl.ttl` (33 node shapes). - `examples/kathmandu-mini-abox.ttl` — a small worked instance dataset (the demonstrator ABox) that exercises every modelling pattern. ### Quality at a glance The ontology is logically consistent under the HermiT reasoner — **0** unsatisfiable classes, both standalone and when merged with CIDOC-CRM, CRMinf, and PROV-O — and answers all **32/32** competency questions as SPARQL queries over the demonstrator ABox (expected-versus-actual exact match). Its external alignment spans SKOS mappings to **11** vocabularies plus the EDM profile. See the [evaluation suite](https://github.com/CAIRNepal/heritagegraphontology/tree/main/evaluation) for reproducible reports.
