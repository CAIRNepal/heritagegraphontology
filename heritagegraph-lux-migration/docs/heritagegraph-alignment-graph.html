About direct URI reuse

Most HeritageGraph classes have their own heritageGraph: URI, related to a CIDOC-CRM or Linked Art term via rdfs:subClassOf. Twelve classes are the exception: for concepts that map 1:1 onto a CIDOC-CRM term — Person, HumanMadeObject, Actor, Place, TimeSpan, Material, Production, DestructionEvent, TransferOfCustody, ConditionAssessment, ConditionState, Technique — the CIDOC-CRM URI (crm:E21_Person, crm:E22_Human-Made_Object, etc.) is used directly as the class, with no separate HeritageGraph wrapper. Those 12 nodes are shown with a dashed ring in this view.
